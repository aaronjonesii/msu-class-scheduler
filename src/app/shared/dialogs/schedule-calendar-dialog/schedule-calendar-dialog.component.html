Getting it into your calendar

Import it into a new calendar. Importing twice can make duplicates, so to change anything, delete that calendar and start again.

  • Google Calendar, on a computer: Settings, then Import & export. Pick your new calendar.
  • Apple Calendar, on a Mac: File, then Import. Pick your new calendar.
  • Outlook, on the web: Add calendar, then Upload from file.
